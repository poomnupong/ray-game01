# Wildwood Keep

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Tablet players exploring and surviving together, without a hardware keyboard.

## Product Purpose
A playable original voxel survival world: gather, craft, swim, build shelter,
share a forest with animals, survive hostile nights, and discover bosses and loot.

## Operating Context
An authoritative local server hosts selectable rooms. Friends on the same Wi-Fi
open the host computer's address in a browser. No account or external service is required.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Touch movement and look must work simultaneously. Inventory is finite. Resources
and crafting costs are real. Room edits, mobs, treasure, and players are shared.
The island is deliberately bounded for tablet performance. Prototype limitations
must be documented honestly. Original art and names only.

## Product Principles
Keyboard-free play; visible consequences; server-owned shared state; readable
controls; a working game rather than a marketing page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large touch targets, portrait and landscape layouts, help available during play,
visible connection errors, and no page gestures interfering with movement.
